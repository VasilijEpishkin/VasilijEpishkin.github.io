HealthOS Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

HealthOS is a personal tool used only by its developer with their own Google account.

Data accessed

With the account owner's consent, HealthOS accesses Google user data (Gmail, Google Drive, Docs, Sheets) of that same account only, to automate the owner's own tasks.

Use, storage and sharing

Data is processed only on the owner's own computer for the owner's own purposes. It is not sold, not shared with third parties, and not used for advertising.

HealthOS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
